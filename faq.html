Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Exam Prep

How do I download and install Exam Prep? +

Download the APK from APKPure or our official website. Once downloaded, tap the APK file on your phone. If your phone asks for permission to install from unknown sources, go to Settings → Security → enable "Install from Unknown Sources." Then follow the installation prompts.

Is Exam Prep really free? +

Yes! Exam Prep is free to download and comes with a 3-day free trial that gives you FULL access to all features. After the trial expires, you can continue using the app with limited access (10 questions per session, 2 flashcards, and only Chapter 1 of novels) or activate for full unlimited access.

How does the 3-day free trial work? +

When you first launch the app, your 3-day trial starts automatically. You get FULL access to all features — CBT simulator, all questions, flashcards, novel summaries, everything. After 3 days, the app switches to limited mode unless you activate.

How do I activate the full version? +

Activation is done entirely within the app. Go to the Activation screen, choose your plan, and follow the checkout process. The app will guide you through the entire activation process.

Does Exam Prep work offline? +

Yes! All questions, study materials, and novel summaries are stored locally on your device. You can study without internet connection. Activation requires internet, but once activated, everything works offline.

What exams does Exam Prep cover? +

Exam Prep covers JAMB UTME, WAEC (SSCE), NECO, and Post UTME for 23+ Nigerian universities. We include past questions, study materials, and recommended novel summaries for all these exams.

Is Exam Prep affiliated with JAMB or WAEC? +

No. Exam Prep is an independent educational app. We are NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JAMB, WAEC, NECO, or any official examination body. All questions and materials are for practice purposes only.

How many questions are in the app? +

We have 50,000+ past questions across JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and Post UTME subjects. Each question includes options, correct answer, and detailed explanation.

Can I use Exam Prep on any Android phone? +

Exam Prep works on Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and higher. It's lightweight and optimized for all screen sizes, including low-end devices.

I lost my activation. What do I do? +

Contact us through the Support page or Telegram. Provide your activation details and we'll help you recover access.

Does the app collect my personal data? +

No. Exam Prep does not collect or share personal data. Your study progress and scores are stored locally on your device only. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
