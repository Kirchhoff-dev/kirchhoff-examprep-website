About Exam Prep

Building the future of exam preparation in Nigeria

Our Story

Exam Prep was born from a simple observation: Nigerian students were struggling to find quality, affordable exam preparation tools. With JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and Post UTME becoming increasingly competitive, students needed more than just textbooks — they needed a smart, accessible, and realistic way to practice.

We built Exam Prep to bridge that gap. Our app brings together 50,000+ past questions, a realistic CBT simulator, novel summaries, study materials, and smart progress tracking — all in one lightweight Android app that works completely offline.

Today, thousands of students across Nigeria use Exam Prep to prepare smarter and score higher. And we're just getting started.

🎯

Our Mission

To make quality exam preparation accessible to every Nigerian student, regardless of their location or internet access.
