Post UTME

Post UTME is the screening exam conducted by Nigerian universities after JAMB. Each school has its own format, subjects, and scoring system. This guide breaks down everything you need to know.

Key Fact: Most schools require you to select 1-4 subjects for Post UTME. The subjects depend on your course and the school's requirements.

How Post UTME Works

  • Format: Computer-Based Test (CBT) at most schools
  • Duration: Usually 30-60 minutes
  • Questions: 20-50 questions depending on the school
  • Subjects: 1-4 subjects based on your course
