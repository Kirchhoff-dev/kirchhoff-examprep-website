WAEC Syllabus Breakdown: All Subjects

Study priorities, key topics, and revision strategies

WAEC SSCE

The WAEC syllabus is your roadmap to success. Knowing what to study — and what to prioritize — can make the difference between a C and an A. This guide breaks down the key topics per subject.

Key Fact: WAEC questions are based STRICTLY on the official syllabus. If it's not in the syllabus, it won't appear in the exam.

Mathematics

High-Priority Topics:

  • Algebra (quadratic equations, simultaneous equations)
  • Geometry (triangles, circles, angles)
  • Trigonometry (sine, cosine, tangent)
  • Statistics & Probability
  • Number Systems (indices, logarithms, surds)

Study Tip: Practice 20+ questions daily. Memorize formulas and learn to apply them quickly.

English Language

High-Priority Topics:

  • Comprehension passages
  • Summary writing
  • Essay writing (formal, informal, narrative)
  • Grammar (tenses, subject-verb agreement)
  • Synonyms & Antonyms
  • Recommended novel (The Lekki Headmaster)

Study Tip: Read the novel summary TWICE. Practice essay outlines for common topics.

Physics

High-Priority Topics:

  • Mechanics (motion, forces, energy)
  • Electricity & Magnetism
  • Waves & Sound
  • Heat & Thermodynamics
  • Optics (light, lenses)

Study Tip: Focus on calculations. Practice past questions by topic.

Chemistry

High-Priority Topics:

  • Atomic Structure & Periodic Table
  • Chemical Bonding
  • Acids, Bases & Salts
  • Organic Chemistry (alkanes, alkenes, alcohols)
  • Stoichiometry (mole calculations)

Study Tip: Memorize the periodic table trends. Practice balancing equations.

Biology

High-Priority Topics:

  • Cell Biology
  • Genetics & Evolution
  • Plant & Animal Physiology
  • Ecology
  • Reproduction

Study Tip: Use diagrams to understand processes. Practice labeling diagrams.

Economics

High-Priority Topics:

  • Demand & Supply
  • Market Structures
  • National Income
  • Money & Banking
  • International Trade

Study Tip: Draw graphs for demand/supply. Understand key definitions.

Government

High-Priority Topics:

  • Constitution & Constitutionalism
  • Separation of Powers
  • Political Parties & Elections
  • Nigerian Government Structure
  • International Organizations (UN, AU, ECOWAS)

Study Tip: Memorize key terms and definitions. Understand Nigerian political history.

Literature in English

High-Priority Topics:

  • Recommended novels (The Lekki Headmaster)
  • Poetry analysis
  • Drama (plays)
  • Literary devices (metaphor, simile, irony)

Study Tip: Read novel summaries carefully. Practice identifying literary devices.

General Study Tips for WAEC

  • Start Early: Don't wait until the last month
  • Use Past Questions: Exam Prep has 50,000+ WAEC questions
  • Practice CBT: Get comfortable with computer-based format
  • Track Progress: Use analytics to identify weak areas
  • Stay Consistent: Study every day, even if just 1 hour
