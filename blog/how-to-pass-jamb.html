How to Pass JAMB in 30 Days

The Ultimate 2025 Study Guide

JAMB UTME

Scoring 280+ in JAMB isn't about studying harder — it's about studying SMARTER. This guide gives you a proven 30-day plan to maximize your score.

Key Fact: JAMB uses Computer-Based Testing (CBT). Practicing with a CBT simulator is ESSENTIAL for success.

Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

Focus on understanding the syllabus and identifying your weak areas.

  • Day 1-2: Download the official JAMB syllabus for all your subjects
  • Day 3-5: Take a diagnostic test to identify weak topics
  • Day 6-7: Create a study schedule — allocate more time to weak areas

Week 2: Intensive Study (Days 8-14)

Now dive deep into your weak topics.

  • Daily: Study 2-3 topics per subject
  • Daily: Practice 50+ questions using Exam Prep CBT simulator
  • Review: Read explanations for every wrong answer
  • Novels: Start reading The Lekki Headmaster summary

Week 3: Practice Mode (Days 15-21)

Shift from studying to practicing.

  • Daily: Take 1 full CBT mock exam (timed)
  • Review: Analyze your score breakdown
  • Focus: Re-study topics where you scored below 60%
  • Speed: Practice answering questions faster

Week 4: Mastery (Days 22-30)

Polish your skills and build confidence.

  • Daily: Take 2 full CBT mock exams
  • Target: Score 80%+ consistently
  • Weak areas: Do final review of difficult topics
  • Rest: Get enough sleep — especially the last 2 days

Subject-Specific Tips

Mathematics

  • Practice 20+ questions daily
  • Memorize formulas (quadratic, trigonometry, probability)
  • Focus on: Algebra, Geometry, Statistics

English Language

  • Read the recommended novel summary TWICE
  • Practice comprehension passages daily
  • Master: Synonyms, Antonyms, Sentence Completion

Physics/Chemistry/Biology

  • Focus on calculations and definitions
  • Practice past questions by topic
  • Memorize key formulas and equations

What to Do on Exam Day

  • Arrive at your CBT center EARLY (at least 1 hour before)
  • Bring your JAMB registration slip
  • Don't panic if the computer acts up — call the invigilator
  • Read each question carefully before answering
  • Manage your time: Don't spend more than 1 minute per question
