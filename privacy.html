Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Important Disclaimer

Exam Prep is an independent educational app. We are NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JAMB, WAEC, NECO, or any official examination body. All questions and materials are for practice purposes only.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Exam Prep. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Exam Prep is designed with privacy first. We collect minimal data:

  • Device ID: Used solely for activation and license verification
  • Activation Data: Your activation code and expiry date (stored locally)
  • Study Progress: Your scores, history, and bookmarks (stored locally on your device)
  • Theme Preference: Light or dark mode setting (stored locally)

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or share:

  • Your name or personal identity
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your browsing history

4. How We Use Your Data

The limited data we collect is used for:

  • Activation: Verifying your license and preventing unauthorized sharing
  • Clock Tamper Detection: Protecting against time manipulation
  • App Functionality: Saving your progress locally

5. Data Storage

All study data (questions, progress, scores, bookmarks) is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your study data to any server.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: For license activation verification only
  • GitHub: For hosting privacy policy and update information

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. Activation codes are bound to your device ID to prevent sharing. All local data is stored in your device's private storage.

8. Children's Privacy

Exam Prep is intended for students preparing for exams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
